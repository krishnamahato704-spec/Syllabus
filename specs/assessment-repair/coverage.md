The catalog contains 73 papers (15+24+34). Within CTET: CDP 60; English 65; Hindi 65; SST 90. English and Hindi each have 20 pedagogy and 45 comprehension items. NCERT has 15 History,20 Geography,15 Civics and 20 Economics. Paper I has 8 questions per unit; NET History has 14 per unit. These are selected concepts, not a complete test of every syllabus item. All papers have unique normalized stems and IDs within a sitting. Difficulty labels are editorial judgements, not measured psychometric difficulty.

## Exam structure

CTET Paper II: 150 questions/marks in 150 minutes, no negative marking. CDP 15 development+5 inclusion+10 learning; English I 15 comprehension+15 pedagogy; Hindi II 15 comprehension+15 pedagogy; SST 40 content+20 pedagogy. English reading has one prose/drama set of 10 and a poem set of 5. Hindi has two prose sets of 8+7. The question allocation within each pair of passages is an app choice. CTET is an OMR examination; this is online practice, not an exact CBT replica.

NET combined:50 Paper I+100 History questions,300 marks,180 minutes, no negative marking. Five Paper I questions per unit follow the syllabus note. Ten questions per History unit is an app balance; no official History unit weighting is claimed. Separate 60/120 minute paper limits and 15–30 minute drills are practice choices. Answered-and-marked responses are scored; unanswered review flags earn zero.

Strictly ancient drills use History units 1–2. Unit 3 has its own early-medieval drill. Units 4–6 map medieval,7–9 modern,10 method. NET subject knowledge does not replace CTET Social Science pedagogy.
